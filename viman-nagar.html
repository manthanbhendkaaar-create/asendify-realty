About the area

Why people rent and buy in Viman Nagar

Viman Nagar is Pune's IT-and-airport belt — home to EON IT Park, close to Phoenix Marketcity, and popular with working professionals who want a short commute. It has a dense mix of studios, 1BHKs, and family-sized apartments, with newer high-rises alongside older, well-established societies.

Studios and 1BHKs here rent quickly among single professionals; 2–3BHK options suit small families wanting to stay close to work and the airport.
