How do I find a flat to rent in Koregaon Park, Pune?

Published 22 October 2026 · Last updated: 22 October 2026 · 4 min read

To find a flat to rent in Koregaon Park, Pune, search the big listing portals (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com and NoBroker) filtered to Koregaon Park, contact a local broker who works the area, and walk the lanes you like, because many good flats are let through society notice boards and word of mouth before they reach a portal. Before you pay anything, visit in person, check who actually owns the flat, get every fee in writing, and register a leave and licence agreement.

Where to look

  • Listing portals. 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com and NoBroker all let you filter by locality, BHK, rent and furnishing. They have the most choice, but the same flat is often listed several times by different brokers, and some listings stay up after the flat is gone.
  • Local brokers. A broker who works Koregaon Park knows which buildings have vacancies and which landlords accept pets, bachelors or company leases. Ask for the brokerage in writing before the first visit.
  • On foot. Lanes off North Main Road and around the Osho garden area have many independent buildings. Ask the society security desk or secretary whether any flats are coming up.

Decide your budget first

A common rule of thumb is to keep rent to about 30% of your monthly take-home pay, or 25% if you want room to save. Then add the cash you need on day one: the security deposit (often several months' rent in Pune, and negotiable), brokerage if you use a broker, the agreement registration cost, and moving costs. What to check on a visit

  1. Water and power. Ask how many hours the building gets municipal water, whether there is a borewell or tanker supply, and whether there is power backup for the lift and the flat.
  2. Parking. Is a covered spot included, and is it allotted to this flat in writing?
  3. Noise. Koregaon Park mixes quiet residential lanes with busy restaurant and nightlife streets. Visit once in the evening, not only in the daytime.
  4. Society rules. Ask about rules on pets, guests, bachelors and move-in times, and who pays the monthly maintenance.
  5. Condition. Note existing damage, fittings and appliances in a written inventory with photos, signed by both sides, so the deposit is not argued over later.

Paperwork and safety

  • Check ownership. Ask to see a document showing the person letting the flat owns it or is authorised to let it, such as the index II, a recent property tax receipt or the society share certificate, and that the name matches their ID.
  • Register the agreement. In Maharashtra, a leave and licence agreement must be registered (Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999). It can be done online through the Department of Registration and Stamps (IGR Maharashtra) e-registration with Aadhaar-based verification. Check the current stamp duty and registration fee on the IGR site.
  • Police verification. Landlords in Pune are required to give tenant details to the police. Pune City Police accepts tenant information online; make sure it is done.
  • Pay traceably. Pay the deposit and rent by bank transfer or UPI to the owner's own account, against a receipt, and only after the agreement is signed. Never pay a "token" to hold a flat you have not seen.

Koregaon Park or nearby?

If Koregaon Park is above budget or you want something quieter, look at Kalyani Nagar across the river and Viman Nagar near the airport. Both have a similar mix of apartments, cafes and offices, with shorter commutes to Kharadi and the airport side of the city.

Key takeaways

  • Search the portals, a local broker and the lanes themselves; many flats never reach a portal.
  • Fix your budget, including the deposit and day-one cash, before you start visiting.
  • Visit in person, in the evening too, and check water, parking, noise and society rules.
  • Verify ownership, register the leave and licence agreement and complete police verification.
  • Pay only traceably, to the owner, after signing.

FAQ

Can I rent in Koregaon Park without a broker?

Yes. Portals such as NoBroker and direct listings on the others connect you with owners, and some flats are let directly through society notice boards. Without a broker you do the checks yourself: ownership, the agreement and police verification.

Is the rental agreement registration compulsory in Pune?

Yes. In Maharashtra a leave and licence agreement has to be registered, and it can be done online through IGR Maharashtra's e-registration.

How much deposit do landlords ask for?

It varies by landlord and flat and is open to negotiation. Agree the amount and the refund terms in the written agreement.
