About the area

Why people rent and buy in Koregaon Park

Koregaon Park is one of Pune's oldest premium residential pockets — tree-lined lanes, low-rise bungalows alongside newer apartment blocks, and easy access to Osho Garden, North Main Road's cafés, and the Pune Cantonment. It suits tenants who want a walkable, established neighbourhood close to the city centre, and buyers looking at long-term value in a locality that has stayed in demand for decades.

Typical rents for a 2BHK in Koregaon Park run in the mid-to-high tens of thousands per month depending on the lane and furnishing; resale apartments trade at a premium reflecting the locality's central position and green cover.
