About the area

Why people rent and buy in Kalyani Nagar

Kalyani Nagar sits along the river close to Pune Airport, and has grown into one of the city's more polished residential belts — gated societies with amenities, proximity to Koregaon Park and Viman Nagar, and quick access to the eastern IT corridor. It's a popular choice for young families and working professionals who want modern construction without moving too far from the airport or the city centre.

3BHK apartments here often command a premium when river-facing or in newer towers; rentals for 2–3BHK units are common among professionals working nearby.
