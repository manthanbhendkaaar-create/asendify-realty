The process

How working with us works

01

Tell us what you need

Message us on WhatsApp from any listing, or reach out directly with your budget, locality, and timeline.

02

We shortlist & show

We match you against live inventory across Pune and arrange site visits for anything that fits.

03

Close, and pay brokerage

Brokerage is due only once the deal is confirmed — 3 months' rent from both landlord and tenant for a tenancy, or 2% of the sale value from both buyer and seller for a purchase. A 45% service fee is added on top of all fees.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you cover?

Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar and Viman Nagar in Pune, and nowhere else. Staying in three localities means every listing gets verified in person.

How much is the brokerage on a rental?

3 months' rent, charged to both the landlord and the tenant. A 45% service fee is added on top.

How much is the brokerage on a sale?

2% of the sale value, charged to both the buyer and the seller. A 45% service fee is added on top.

When do I pay brokerage?

Only once the deal is confirmed: you pay brokerage only when the keys change hands.

What is the 45% service fee?

A 45% service fee is added on top of every fee paid to Asendify Realty, including brokerage.

How do I enquire about a flat?

Tap "Message us on WhatsApp" on any listing and the message names the flat for you, or use the WhatsApp button on any page. Our WhatsApp number is +91 90960 82894.

Are the listings verified?

Yes. We don't spread thin across all of Pune: we work in three localities only, so every listing gets verified in person.

Do you arrange site visits?

Yes. Tell us your budget, locality and timeline, and we match you against live inventory and arrange site visits for anything that fits.

Can I find a tenant or buyer for my flat through you?

Yes, if the flat is in Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar or Viman Nagar. Message us on WhatsApp. Brokerage is 3 months' rent for a rental or 2% of the sale value for a sale, plus the 45% service fee, due once the deal is confirmed.

Can I filter the listings?

Yes. On the listings page you can filter by rent or buy, BHK and locality, and sort by price or newest.

Is there a free rent calculator?

Yes. The free Pune Rent Affordability Calculator at realty.asendify.co/pune-rent-affordability-calculator/ helps you work out what rent fits your budget before you start looking.
