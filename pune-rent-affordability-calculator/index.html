Keep rent at or below 30% of your monthly take-home pay, or 25% if you want to save more. On ₹1,00,000 a month that is up to ₹30,000 (30%) or ₹25,000 (25%). Then budget for the security deposit, brokerage and first month's rent upfront.

Questions

How much rent can I afford in Pune?

A common rule of thumb is to keep rent at or below 30% of your monthly income, or 25% if you want more room to save. On ₹1,00,000 a month that is up to ₹30,000 (30%) or ₹25,000 (25%). These are general guides, not rules from any bank or landlord.

Should I use my gross salary or take-home pay?

Take-home (in-hand) pay is the safer choice, because it is the money you actually have each month after tax and PF. Using gross salary gives a higher figure that can feel tight in practice.

How much cash do I need upfront to rent a flat in Pune?

Add the security deposit, any brokerage, the first month's rent and one-off costs such as agreement registration and moving. Deposits and brokerage vary by landlord and broker, so enter the numbers you have been quoted; the calculator adds them up for you.

What brokerage does Asendify Realty charge for a rental?

Asendify Realty's brokerage for a rental is 3 months' rent from both the landlord and the tenant, plus a 45% service fee on top, due only once the deal is confirmed. For a tenant that is 4.35 months' rent in total. The calculator has a button to use this figure.

Do my EMIs change how much rent I can afford?

Yes. Rent and loan EMIs both come out of the same income. Enter your monthly EMIs and the calculator shows how much is left each month after rent and EMIs at each rent level.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is general guidance and simple arithmetic on the numbers you enter. Your own budget, savings goals and family needs matter more than any rule of thumb.
